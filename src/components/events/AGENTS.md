# San Juan Bowl event pages and assets

- San Juan Bowl micro extracts clone unchanged source shapes with inherited styles and coordinates; its client-loaded Three.js viewer orbits the stack midpoint — why: fused silhouettes must not be invented, and layer spread must not move the camera target.
- San Juan Bowl major-piece exports wrap unchanged detail paths in one named group per piece; viewer lettering assets and exports use the same original shape indices, and micro view is optional — why: grouping and separate lettering selection must preserve approved geometry without duplicating ribbon details.

- San Juan Bowl component assets preserve original top-level SVG drawing order and source coordinates, and count every shape element (path, polygon, polyline — 293 total); `san-juan-logo-measurements.json` records measured geometry and feeds the production calculator — why: exact inspection must not redraw composite shapes or confuse artboard width with badge width.

- San Juan Bowl guide pages live in `src/components/events/sjb-pages/` and render under both the app routes and the public `/share/san-juan-bowl/*` routes; links between them go through `SjbLink`, which keeps share visitors inside the share link — why: outsiders see the guides without the Element menu or links into the app.
- The San Juan Bowl lettering spec page (`sjb-pages/LetteringSpecPage.tsx`, `/events/san-juan-bowl/brand-kit/lettering` + `/share/san-juan-bowl/lettering`) reads every number from `src/lib/sjb-lettering-spec.json` and shows the supplied drawings in `public/downloads/sjb-lettering/spec/`; both were generated from the same exact construction as `sjb-lettering-exact.svg` — why: spec, drawings and master must never drift, so replace all three together and never hand-edit values or redraw.
- San Juan Bowl stacked lettering (`SjbLetteringStacked.tsx`, data `src/lib/sjb-lettering-stacked.json`, files `public/downloads/sjb-lettering/stacked/`: 7 layouts × 3 colourways matching the single-line colourways) is generated from the same exact construction: letters are exact copies of the master, lines are only translated (line gap 29), and the full-width BOWL is the master scaled by exactly 13/8 with its letter gaps solved to span SAN JUAN — why: stacked and single-line lettering must stay the same artwork; regenerate, never edit by hand.
- San Juan Bowl small-size mark (`SJB_SMALL_MARK`, files `public/downloads/sjb-mark/`) is the badge frame × 3/4 around the 3-line stacked lettering at scale 1 (translated only, equal 69-unit clearance), and the lockups (`SJB_LOCKUPS`, `public/downloads/sjb-lockups/`: team matchup, official partner, badge + venue/date) copy the supplied badge in untouched with x clear space and live Geist text — why: both were chosen by the brand lead from options; regenerate rather than edit, and never put the small mark beside the full badge.
- San Juan Bowl (`san-juan-bowl`) is a built-in event entry with its own data module `src/lib/san-juan-bowl.ts` and brand kit at `/events/san-juan-bowl/brand-kit`; logo files are CDN asset pointers in `src/assets/san-juan-bowl/` from the supplied FINAL_LOGO_26 package — why: venue and dates come from the confirmed `SJB_EVENT` facts, and the artwork is never redrawn.
- The San Juan Bowl brand guide PDF is built by `scripts/sjb-brand-guide-pdf.py` with `VERSION`/`RELEASED`/`HISTORY` constants; each rebuild bumps the version, is uploaded as `san-juan-bowl-brand-guide-v<version>.pdf` and replaces `src/assets/sjb/brand-guide.pdf.asset.json` and both page links — why: asset links are fixed copies, so a changed file needs a new named version.
- The Bowl master logo kit ZIP (`src/assets/sjb/master-logo-kit.zip.asset.json`) carries the same version as the brand guide PDF and is rebuilt with it — why: the kit and guide must never disagree.
- San Juan Bowl stadium render plates live in `src/lib/sjb-stadium-renders.ts` (SJB_STADIUM_RENDERS): six clean cinematic night-game backgrounds (16:9, 2:1, 4:5, 3:4, 9:16, 1:1) with Canva media IDs, no text/logos — why: one reusable background set across all Bowl design sets instead of per-design one-offs.
- Bowl social gallery previews re-pulled from Canva live at `public/sjb-canva/live/<designId>.png` and are listed in `SJB_CANVA_LIVE_PREVIEW_IDS` (`src/lib/sjb-canva-live.ts`), which overrides older snapshot paths — why: one refresh path keyed by Canva design id, so stale snapshots cannot shadow a newer Canva edit.
- San Juan Bowl digital billboards (`/events/san-juan-bowl/billboards` + share) are rendered from the real shield by `scripts/sjb-billboards.py` into `public/sjb-billboards/`; in-scene photos are generated magenta-screen scenes with the real board warped on by `scripts/sjb-billboard-mockups.py`, always labelled illustrative; editable Canva masters are listed in `SJB_BILLBOARD_CANVA`; scenes are refreshed by re-running the mockup script (the in-page updater is no longer shown) — why: AI never redraws the logo or copy, and mockups are never mistaken for real locations.
