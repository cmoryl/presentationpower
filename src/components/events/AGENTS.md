# San Juan Bowl event pages and assets

- Bowl script rules (brand guide PDF, digital billboards, Illustrator masters) live in `scripts/AGENTS.md`.
- Campaign design-asset panels resolve reusable four-page Canva masters and cleaned per-page pictures/ZIP pointers through `sjb-campaign-sequence-masters`; these are not per-post calendar sequences — why: asset masters must stay separate from finished post registries and preserve original single-page files.

- Sequence companion grounds resolve by look, page role and size through `sjbSequenceGround` in `sjb-sequence-backgrounds` (Light tickets use the user-supplied rising-football art, metadata-cleaned, with copy kept in its white sky); only drawn headline/details/tickets pages use them, while original cover imagery and saved Canva pages remain intact — why: coordinated AI-edited grounds must not replace issued art or change campaign identity.

- Drawn Bowl social callouts use SjbFittedCallout to measure the loaded campaign font against their reserved width and height; saved Canva images remain untouched — why: large headlines must fit actual lettering without stretching or overwriting approved originals.
- Set 2 drawn cover reflows follow the approved lowered Feed/Square composition in CampaignCover, with square conference marks enlarged proportionally and separated horizontally from the shield; other looks retain their own composition — why: this correction must reach missing-export demos without changing unrelated campaigns or pretending saved Canva originals were updated.

- Bowl calendar multi-page formats (LinkedIn 4:5 and square carousels, Instagram carousel, Story sequence) show a labelled demo sequence until real Canva pages exist: `src/lib/sjb-calendar-demo.ts` + `src/components/events/SjbDemoSequence.tsx` draw four pages (logo-only cover, headline + subheadline, details, tickets) per post × look × format from the post's own headline and `SJB_EVENT` facts only, exported through the shared asset exporter as a PDF or a ZIP at the format's exact pixels — why: the calendar must read as planned work without passing a placeholder off as approved artwork, so every page keeps its DEMO LAYOUT badge and the cover only borrows a cleaned single image when one is registered.
- San Juan Bowl micro extracts clone unchanged source shapes with inherited styles and coordinates; its client-loaded Three.js viewer orbits the stack midpoint — why: fused silhouettes must not be invented, and layer spread must not move the camera target.
- San Juan Bowl major-piece exports wrap unchanged detail paths in one named group per piece; viewer lettering assets and exports use the same original shape indices, and micro view is optional — why: grouping and separate lettering selection must preserve approved geometry without duplicating ribbon details.

- San Juan Bowl component assets preserve original top-level SVG drawing order and source coordinates, and count every shape element (path, polygon, polyline — 293 total); `san-juan-logo-measurements.json` records measured geometry and feeds the production calculator — why: exact inspection must not redraw composite shapes or confuse artboard width with badge width.

- San Juan Bowl guide pages live in `src/components/events/sjb-pages/` and render under both the app routes and the public `/share/san-juan-bowl/*` routes; links between them go through `SjbLink`, which keeps share visitors inside the share link — why: outsiders see the guides without the Element menu or links into the app.
- The San Juan Bowl lettering spec page (`sjb-pages/LetteringSpecPage.tsx`, `/events/san-juan-bowl/brand-kit/lettering` + `/share/san-juan-bowl/lettering`) reads every number from `src/lib/sjb-lettering-spec.json` and shows the supplied drawings in `public/downloads/sjb-lettering/spec/`; both were generated from the same exact construction as `sjb-lettering-exact.svg` — why: spec, drawings and master must never drift, so replace all three together and never hand-edit values or redraw.
- San Juan Bowl stacked lettering (`SjbLetteringStacked.tsx`, data `src/lib/sjb-lettering-stacked.json`, files `public/downloads/sjb-lettering/stacked/`: 7 layouts × 3 colourways matching the single-line colourways) is generated from the same exact construction: letters are exact copies of the master, lines are only translated (line gap 29), and the full-width BOWL is the master scaled by exactly 13/8 with its letter gaps solved to span SAN JUAN — why: stacked and single-line lettering must stay the same artwork; regenerate, never edit by hand.
- San Juan Bowl small-size mark (`SJB_SMALL_MARK`, files `public/downloads/sjb-mark/`) is the badge frame × 3/4 around the 3-line stacked lettering at scale 1 (translated only, equal 69-unit clearance), and the lockups (`SJB_LOCKUPS`, `public/downloads/sjb-lockups/`: team matchup, official partner, badge + venue/date) copy the supplied badge in untouched with x clear space and live Geist text — why: both were chosen by the brand lead from options; regenerate rather than edit, and never put the small mark beside the full badge.
- San Juan Bowl (`san-juan-bowl`) is a built-in event entry with its own data module `src/lib/san-juan-bowl.ts` and brand kit at `/events/san-juan-bowl/brand-kit`; logo files are CDN asset pointers in `src/assets/san-juan-bowl/` from the supplied FINAL_LOGO_26 package — why: venue and dates come from the confirmed `SJB_EVENT` facts, and the artwork is never redrawn.
- The Bowl master logo kit ZIP (`src/assets/sjb/master-logo-kit.zip.asset.json`) carries the same version as the brand guide PDF and is rebuilt with it — why: the kit and guide must never disagree.
- San Juan Bowl stadium render plates live in `src/lib/sjb-stadium-renders.ts` (SJB_STADIUM_RENDERS): six clean cinematic night-game backgrounds (16:9, 2:1, 4:5, 3:4, 9:16, 1:1) with Canva media IDs, no text/logos — why: one reusable background set across all Bowl design sets instead of per-design one-offs.
- Bowl social gallery previews re-pulled from Canva live at `public/sjb-canva/live/<designId>.png` and are listed in `SJB_CANVA_LIVE_PREVIEW_IDS` (`src/lib/sjb-canva-live.ts`), which overrides older snapshot paths — why: one refresh path keyed by Canva design id, so stale snapshots cannot shadow a newer Canva edit.
- San Juan Bowl colour separations (`src/lib/sjb-colour-separations.ts`, plate files in `src/assets/san-juan-bowl/separations/`, section `SjbColourSeparations.tsx` shown as guide section 05) are split from the supplied full-colour master by resolving that file's own ink classes, so every plate holds the master's shapes at source coordinates in source drawing order; ink codes are read from `SJB_COLORS`, the pack is rebuilt from the master, and the plates are labelled ink separations — not CMYK process separations, and the 33 unpainted contours stay out — why: a recoloured, re-flowed or hand-made plate would print a badge nobody approved.
- Bowl calendar multi-page Canva files are listed per look/post/size in `src/lib/sjb-calendar-canva-sequences.json` (read via `sjbCalendarSequenceCanvaUrl`): page 1 is a shared logo-only cover per look/size, page 2 the post's saved single ad (headline + subheadline), pages 3–4 one shared details + tickets pair per size (announced facts + Ticketera only); the 4:5 file serves both LinkedIn 4:5 and Instagram carousel — why: live carousels must reuse approved covers and never invent per-post copy; previews stay demo-labelled until the pages are exported.
- Saved multi-page pictures (real calendar sequences and the four-page campaign masters) display through `SjbSequenceViewer` as a carousel or story; a master stands in for a post's sequence only for post 01, whose headline it carries — why: real pages must show in platform layouts without passing post 01's headline off as another post.
- Set 2 and Set 4 post players resolve through `sjb-post-sequences`: untouched shared three-page Canva exports provide cover/details/tickets, while page 2 reads that post's current native single; `SjbSavedSequence` bundles those same bytes into ZIP/PDF and links source documents separately — why: every post plays its own artwork without pretending an assembled export is a newly saved multi-page Canva document.
- Canva → app picture sync (`canva-sync.server.ts`, admin panel `SjbCanvaSyncPanel`): edit times are checked first and only designs edited after their last saved picture (bundled pointer `created_at` or `canva_asset_sync.synced_at`) are exported, metadata-stripped by `strip-image-metadata.ts`, stored in private `canva-sync` and served via `/api/public/canva-sync/$designId?v=`; `useCanvaSynced` overrides bundled pictures — why: Canva has no edit webhook and a daily export allowance, and bundled pointers cannot be rewritten at runtime. Each sync verifies the saved copy, the live link and a Canva re-check, and reports results by post/look/size with plain errors from `canva-sync-messages.ts`.
- Canva picture sync runs daily via the managed schedule calling `/api/public/hooks/canva-sync` (`runBackgroundSync`: ≤120 checks, ≤25 exports per run, stops on allowance limits, logs to `canva_sync_runs`); health shows on Admin → Canva connection, and the calendar panel prints the latest check time plus the last run's counts through `lastRunSummary`/`describeLastRun` — why: large edit days must catch up without manual clicks or wasted exports, and readers must see when the site last looked at Canva.
- The Canva picture sync watches every design in `canva-sync-targets.ts` (calendar singles as page-1 JPEG, selected billboards as PNG, carousel frame docs and four-page masters as every page); multi-page copies are served with `?page=N` and `useCanvaSynced(id, fallback, page)`; bundled ZIP/PDF packs are not rebuilt by the sync — why: one watch list, and billboard downloads must stay PNG.
- Bowl attendee badge and T-shirt comps (`/events/san-juan-bowl/badges`, `/tshirts`) are drawn from `src/lib/sjb-merch-comps.ts` with supplied logos placed unchanged and facts only from `SJB_EVENT`; badges reuse the NEXT plastic badge size from `BADGE_SPEC` — why: comps are directions to pick finals from, never print masters or invented facts.
- Bowl sequence ZIP/PDF downloads are rebuilt in the browser at click time from the same URLs the players show (Canva sync first, bundled original otherwise) via `sjb-sequence-pack` / `SjbPackButton`; static pack files are no longer linked — why: packs must never lag behind refreshed pictures.
- Bowl T-shirt pictures are asset pointers in `src/assets/sjb-shirts/`, grouped on the page by filename prefix (`set-`, `design-`, `colour-`, `role-`, `distressed-`, people prefixes) in `SjbShirtPhotos`; artwork is composited from the exact Canva/logo files onto AI-made blanks and scenes, never redrawn by AI — why: demo shirts must show the real designs while the people and places stay labelled as examples.
- The "Worn at the game" people photos are switched off the T-shirts page through `SHOW_PEOPLE` in `SjbShirtPhotos`; their pointers and prefix grouping stay in place — why: the section is paused for review, not deleted, so it must be reversible without rebuilding assets.
- Basic studio model sheets pair `studio-<set filename>` asset pointers with existing flat sets; `scripts/sjb-shirt-studio.py` uses exact supplied art on measured reusable blanks — why: every set retains its identity across man/woman/child front/back views without reviving paused game-day photos.
- Every Bowl page (app and share) opens with `SjbMasthead` (stadium plate, grit, Light-social Barlow Condensed lettering, `SjbSiteNav` section tabs; event home hero carries the same nav); SjbShell pages draw it inside their shell and the withSjbTrail copy hides via CSS, and Bowl page h1/h2 follow the same lettering — why: the Bowl pages must read as one micro-site inside Element.
- The Set 5 design-asset pages (`/events/san-juan-bowl/set-5-assets`, `/share/san-juan-bowl/set-5-assets`) are deleted along with their `SjbMasthead` section tab, trail label, share `SjbPage` key, Recent-updates card and the Social page's "View design assets" link; the `src/lib/sjb-set5-*.ts` registries and `src/assets/sjb-set5-*` artwork pointers stay in the repo unreferenced — why: the brand lead dropped the page but the artwork must remain recoverable without re-exporting from Canva.
