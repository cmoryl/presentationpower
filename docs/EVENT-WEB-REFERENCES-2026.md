## Preservation and rights

Thirteen originals and thirteen viewing thumbnails are stored in the existing private event-photos bucket. Catalogue: `src/lib/event-web-references.json`, carrying source post, source date, dimensions, checksum, event association and rights state. Signed-in event homes and supplied-kit pages display the same catalogue through `EventWebReferences` with short-lived viewing links and enlarged inspection.

All rights are **unconfirmed** and marketing clearance is **false**. Public availability, a company publisher or a participant post is not a reuse licence. No images were placed on a public CDN, in public shares, PDFs, downloadable brand packs or marketing pickers. Publication requires photographer/rights-owner permission and applicable participant releases. Web references are observational evidence only, not approved venue facts, logo masters or surveyed sign placements.

## Coverage limits

This is a targeted multi-source reference search, not a complete photographer archive. Registration desks, floor wayfinding, charging-station installations, full badge sets and precise sign dimensions remain missing unless present in supplied artwork. Social page-reader failures were followed by direct browser inspection; previews retrieved successfully, full videos were not audited. No event outcomes, attendance totals or approval decisions were invented.
