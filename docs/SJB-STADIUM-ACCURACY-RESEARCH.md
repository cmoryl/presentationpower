# Stadium location accuracy — 14 November 2026

## Requirement and status
The user requires 100% location accuracy. The current source-map model does not meet this requirement. It is archived separately rather than silently relabelled as accurate. No geographic/architectural certification is issued, including for the existing BoothHUB model.

## Official document retrieved and read
- National Park Service record: https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/NRIS/13001118
- Full nomination PDF (8,949,020 bytes): https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/18713893-5cc8-4a5b-bda1-31f37e6add0c/
- Printed page 3: northwest–southeast axis; northwest entrance; Roberto Clemente Coliseum west; FDR Avenue north and Luis A. Ferré highway east. This contradicts treating the generated map as geographic evidence.
- Printed pages 4–5: rear T-shaped supporting elements; cantilevered concrete canopy made of hyperbolic-parabolic slabs, approximately 2.5 inches thick historically; two concourses and suspended U-shaped press/VIP clubhouse. Current procedural geometry does not reproduce those structures exactly.
- Printed page 5: four inclined roof-mounted lighting towers plus four freestanding outfield towers; historical baseball distances 325/404 ft and 8-ft fence, not approved football clearance/alignment.
- Figure 1 site image, Figure 7 aerial and 2013 mapped boundary provide historical reference, not a current venue survey. Text-extracted UTM values require checking against the scanned map before any georeferencing; do not paste them into production as verified coordinates.

## Missing evidence
Current georeferenced venue footprint/as-built drawings or licensed survey mesh; elevation/roof sections and verified surroundings; approved football placement, sideline clearances and event footprints. Public research can constrain an estimated model but cannot establish 100% accuracy. Supplied field raster remains provisional visual artwork, not a survey.
