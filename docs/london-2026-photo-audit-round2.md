# London 2026 photo audit — round 2 (Google Photos album, 301 photos)

Source: shared album "2026 TransPerfectNEXT (London)", added to the London photo library as "Event highlights (Google Photos)". Photo numbers (#n) match file names GooglePhotos-000n. AI-assisted reading of each photo; sizes and heights below are recommendations, not measurements.

This audit is based on 287 per-photo observations from the event at the QEII Centre. It identifies successes to replicate and problems to fix for the upcoming San Francisco edition.

## 1. What Worked

The event design was largely successful, with a strong, consistent, and highly legible brand presence.

*   **Consistent & Legible Core Branding:** The main 'TransPerfect NEXT' and 'BEYOND INTELLIGENCE' lockups were applied with excellent consistency and clarity across major assets, including entrance headers (#2, #4, #9, #139), room backdrops (#16, #21, #22, #52, #90, #135, #271), and registration walls (#29, #147, #164). The use of large white text on a purple/blue gradient provided excellent contrast.
*   **Effective Outdoor Wayfinding:** Tall, vertical flags were used effectively to brand the exterior of the venue and guide attendees from a distance (#4, #5, #63, #113, #114, #115, #116, #137). The main digital banner over the entrance was large, bright, and clearly identified the event (#2, #4, #78, #123, #139).
*   **Clear Staff & Track Identification:** The red 'ASK ME ANYTHING' sashes worn by staff were highly visible and effective (#14, #66, #73, #81, #87). The use of different coloured and branded lanyards for different event tracks (e.g., 'MediaNEXT' #54, 'LearnNEXT' #91, 'Legal NEXT' #104, #285) was a successful way to segment attendees.
*   **High-Quality Registration & Check-in:** The registration area was a highlight, featuring large, clearly branded counters and bright, legible overhead digital screens to direct attendees to the correct track or desk (#29, #30, #40, #86, #100, #162).
*   **Professional Collateral & Badges:** Printed materials like the event program showed high print quality, with sharp text and vibrant colours (#20, #57, #152, #154). Attendee badges were clearly printed with large names and consistent branding, and lanyards were colourful and legible (#27, #144, #181, #191, #243, #258).

## 2. Problems Found, Ranked by Impact

### 1. (High Impact) Legibility Lost to Lighting & Poor Contrast

*   **Problem:** Key branded assets were rendered difficult to read by ambient lighting. Direct sun caused glare on the main outdoor banner (#71, #77), strong internal spotlights washed out a large hanging 'BEYOND INTELLIGENCE' sign (#190), and coloured atmospheric lighting reduced the contrast of backdrop text in some areas (#121, #122). In one case, a blue logo on a dark background lacked contrast (#95).
*   **Fix for San Francisco:**
    *   Specify matte-finish materials for all large-format outdoor signage to reduce daytime glare.
    *   During setup, test all major indoor signs under their final show lighting conditions.
    *   Where coloured show lighting hits a printed ground, agree a neutral light state for that wall rather than changing the artwork (logos are never keylined).

### 2. (Medium Impact) Information Obscured or Too Small

*   **Problem:** Some information was difficult to access. A digital screen with agenda information was placed low and was partially obscured by people standing in front of it (#259). One version of the attendee badge included wayfinding information, but the text was noted as being very small and only legible on close inspection (#28), making it impractical for quick glances in a crowd.
*   **Fix for San Francisco:**
    *   Mount all key informational screens and directional signage above head height (minimum 2 metres).
    *   Simplify badges to focus on name, company, and track/role. Move detailed schedules and maps exclusively to the mobile app and a few large, dedicated 'You Are Here' map displays in high-traffic areas.

### 3. (Low Impact) Minor Production Errors

*   **Problem:** A minor production error was observed where a 'TRANS PERFECT NEXT' logo was cropped at the bottom edge of a vertical banner (#145). While not critical, this undermines the otherwise professional execution.
*   **Fix for San Francisco:**
    *   Institute a mandatory sign-off checklist for the event manager to physically inspect all printed assets (banners, backdrops, signs) upon delivery to the venue, before they are installed. This allows time to request a reprint if errors are found.

## 3. Asset-by-Asset Notes

*   **Backdrops:** Overwhelmingly positive. Large, seamless, and highly legible prints were the standard (#16, #21, #90, #135, #164, #271). The only issue noted was a loss of contrast when washed with coloured light (#121, #122).
*   **Kiosks/Booths:** Generally well-branded with clear, eye-level logos and text (#26, #50, #93, #170, #214, #235). The large cylindrical 'INNOVATION LOUNGE' structure was a particular success as a central landmark (#26, #213). **Weak evidence:** Very few straight-on shots of booths were available; one overhead photo (#49) was insufficient for a full audit.
*   **Registration:** A clear strength. The combination of a boldly branded main counter and clear overhead digital screens for different tracks was highly effective and consistently praised in observations (#29, #30, #40, #86, #100, #150, #162).
*   **Headers/Wayfinding:** Excellent. Outdoor flags provided a great sense of arrival (#4, #115, #116), and the main entrance header was unmissable (#2, #10, #139). Some issues with glare on the outdoor banner (#77) and a washed-out indoor hanging sign (#190) were the only weaknesses.
*   **Screens:** Generally bright, high-resolution, and legible (#14, #47, #73, #97, #156). Minor issues included some glare (#23, #24) and one screen being placed too low and getting blocked by attendees (#259).
*   **Stage:** The main stage sets featured large, high-resolution digital screens and crisp side-panel branding that were clear and professional (#148, #149, #151, #153, #198). No significant issues were observed.
*   **Collateral:** Print quality was high across programs, tabletop signs, and branded items like water bottles and bags (#20, #67, #109, #152, #154, #266). QR codes were used effectively (#67, #87). The observation that one badge contained too much small text (#28) suggests a need for simplification.
*   **Badges:** Attendee names were large and clear. Division-specific coloured lanyards (e.g., 'GlobalLink NEXT' #28, 'LearnNEXT' #92, 'Legal NEXT' #104) were well-executed. Branding was consistent and legible (#27, #144, #181, #243, #258).
