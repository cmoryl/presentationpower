SAN JUAN BOWL lettering specification

The construction rules behind SJB-Lettering-Exact.svg. Every letter is built from the same small set of numbers, every coordinate is an exact decimal, and nothing is rounded. Values are in units of u = cap height ÷ 120. Pink lines are dimensions.

4 · Serifs, corners and the shadow

The shadow runs at exactly 45°, the same angle as every corner cut. So a lower-left cut casts no shadow at all instead of a hairline. The serif size is set by the shadow so that no shadow ever touches or nearly touches another shadow or a serif tip.

9 · File and editing rules

  • File: SJB-Lettering-Exact.svg. Groups SAN, JUAN and BOWL, then one group per letter holding Shadow-X under Face-X. No strokes, masks, clips or transforms. Straight lines only.
  • Scale uniformly by cap height. Never stretch one axis, never redraw single letters, never recolour the shadow separately from the system.
  • Kern by moving a whole letter group (shadow and face together). Keep the 12 / 29 face-to-face rhythm.
  • Exactness: every coordinate is a multiple of 1/256 and is stored exactly in Illustrator and browsers. Avoid "simplify path" and snapping to pixel grid, which break it.
  • Changing a rule (stroke, serif, shadow) means regenerating all letters from the construction, not editing points by hand. The shadow is always the full sweep of the face.
