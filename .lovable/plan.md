# Editable San Juan Bowl application library

Replace the three illustrative application areas in the Bowl brand kit with real, individually previewed templates, live Canva links and editable downloads. Keep the existing Social examples unchanged.

## Templates to create

### Presentations & documents
- **Presentation kit:** 16:9, with eight reusable layouts: cover, section opener, agenda, text/image, comparison, timeline, partner placement and closing. Editable PowerPoint and live Canva versions.
- **Letterhead:** US Letter and A4, editable Word and fixed-page Canva versions.
- **Event brief / fact sheet:** US Letter and A4, editable Word and Canva versions.
- **Report:** coordinated cover and interior pages in US Letter and A4, editable Word and Canva versions.

### Signage & fabrication
- **Poster:** 18×24 inches and A3.
- **Directional sign:** 24×18 inches, with editable arrow, destination and secondary text.
- **Tabletop sign:** 5×7 inches and A5.
- **Pull-up banner:** a proposed 33×80-inch artwork layout, explicitly subject to the supplier’s hardware template.
- **Backlit / vinyl artwork:** separate editable artwork layers and a supplier handoff sheet. Physical dimensions, mounting, cut paths and material requirements remain unapproved until supplied.

Each sign receives a live Canva design and an Illustrator-compatible layered SVG source. Include trim, bleed and safe-area information separately from the artwork. Do not mislabel SVG as a native .ai file or label any file print-approved.

### AV & motion
- **Holding screen:** 1920×1080 and 3840×2160.
- **Speaker title / lower third:** 1920×1080 with separate logo, name, role and background elements.
- **Opening / closing sequence:** editable widescreen Canva timeline and PowerPoint source, plus a rendered MP4 preview where export is available.
- **Portrait screen:** 1080×1920, with matching opening and closing frames.

Screen templates include explicit safe-area overlays that are excluded from final output. AV frame rates and delivery settings are identified rather than described as universally broadcast-certified.

## Artwork and content
- Use the supplied Bowl logos unchanged: full colour on light, approved white-outline artwork on dark.
- Use the established Bowl palette and Geist, without introducing another brand style or borrowing NEXT templates.
- Include SANJUANBOWL.COM on the templates. Populate only announced event facts from the existing Bowl record.
- Unknown names, agenda items, sponsor placements and outcomes are visibly marked editable fields, never invented claims.
- Produce coordinated light and dark presentation/AV layouts; documents prioritise readable light interiors.

## Brand-kit experience
- Replace the generic studies in **Signage & fabrication**, **AV & motion** and **Presentations & documents** with real preview cards.
- Each card identifies its exact size, editable formats, Canva link and download options.
- Group variants under their template rather than creating a wall of duplicate cards.
- Use the shared Bowl content on both signed-in and public brand-kit pages. No links to NEXT tools.
- Keep source downloads separate from proofs and rendered video.

## Verification and limits
- Inspect every generated slide/page and each sign/AV format for logo clear space, clipping, readable type, alignment and correct dimensions.
- Validate PowerPoint and Word packages; verify live Canva links and actual downloads through the page.
- A PowerPoint schema check is not a claim that Microsoft Office has opened the file.
- Physical signage requires supplier measurements, material specifications and a proof before production approval; do not invent these or silently convert RGB to CMYK.
- Canva’s previously reported daily export limit may block exports. Keep affected templates explicitly pending rather than substituting pictures or claiming completed downloads.
- Canva edits requiring approval will be presented together for review before saving; original social artwork remains unchanged.

## Technical implementation
- Add one Bowl application-template registry shared by previews, Canva links and static downloadable asset pointers.
- Extend the existing shared application-gallery component with optional real-template content; other brands retain their current examples.
- Generate fixed files in the sandbox, validate them, then register them as app assets. Do not add a new in-app editor or server-side generation workflow.
- Keep source-generation scripts and focused registry tests in the project so templates can be rebuilt consistently.
